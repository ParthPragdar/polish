# Community guidelines

Polish welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, specific, and constructive when discussing code or behavior. Assume good intent, accept correction, and focus disagreements on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, spam, and posting another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not share private drafts, credentials, or identifying screenshots in project discussions.

These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and project discussions. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so would not expose private information or create further harm.

Report inappropriate public content with GitHub's reporting tools, or contact a maintainer privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish private evidence or retaliate against someone who reports a concern.
